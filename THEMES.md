# Themes

Durable strategic pillars ZirekHQ's work sits under. A theme rarely closes;
initiatives under it come and go.

## Screen Reader Access

Making screen readers usable in under-served languages -- starting with
Kurmanji NVDA localisation (`nvdaku`).

## Local Speech Synthesis

Fully local, no-cloud neural text-to-speech for languages large TTS vendors
don't serve -- the `sonata`/`dengjen-tts` stack and its dependencies
(`tqsm`, `libtashkeel`).

## Sustainable Contribution

Turning "help wanted" into work volunteers actually sustain. Native-speaker
review time is the org's real bottleneck, not tooling -- this theme is
about the commitment/engagement layer that makes volunteer hours durable,
not one-off.

- **Initiative:** [Dengjen Werger](https://github.com/ZirekHQ/dengjen-werger)
  -- commitment and peer-review layer for Kurmanji translation contribution.
